# Lessons: failure modes from past runs and the rule each one produced

Append-only. One entry per lesson: date, what happened, the rule. New entries go at the bottom with the date. These are the reasons behind the hard rules in SKILL.md; when a rule looks arbitrary, the lesson is here.

## Research errors

- 2026-10-26: a dig wrote "$NVDA still 0 hits" in the stock-ticker meta; the pack stage found a live coin at six figures under that ticker. Rule: every EMPTY verdict is rechecked by VERIFY with a fresh search before output, and the pack stage rechecks again before writing "mint".
- 2026-10-26: the "cleanest new CA" on a viral pet story rugged 99% within the hour after it was written up; a second fresh CA with millions in hourly volume on six-figure mcap dumped 84% in five minutes. Rule: under-1h CAs always carry "expect 50% wicks"; the next run opens with a correction; volume far above liquidity is written as wash, not as strength.
- 2026-10-26: an "Anthropic IPO" runner went from a few million to a few thousand in fifteen minutes, hours after the desk called it the pick of the hour on the same event. Rule: event-name coins that bond on a headline are momentum trades; the dig says "momentum only" and gives the mint ideas instead of the bag.
- 2026-10-26: a dog in a four-second clip was traded under a name the poster never gave. Rule: unconfirmed names are labeled unconfirmed and ranked below confirmed names; the bio for a satellite must hold whatever the real name turns out to be.
- 2026-10-26: a platform coin ran on "Shaw" association although the person never confirmed a link; a tabletop-game writer hosted a community space and then posted that his involvement had nothing to do with crypto. Rule: endorsement and disavowal are checked before any person-linked coin is called; a disavowal means momentum only and no front-run of that person's name.
- 2026-10-25: a Solana brand clone of a non-transferable HyperEVM token was dead on arrival. Rule: search the word on every chain and report where the original lives and whether it is transferable.
- 2026-10-27: a chihuahua "GTA6 dog" ran to seven figures on invented lore while a real in-game brand on a physical billboard was still open. Rule: invented lore can run, but the desk recommends the real receipt (physical ad, official page) as the mint, and labels invented lore as invented.

## Timing errors

- 2026-10-25: a front-run list sequenced a launch for "tonight or Monday morning" without converting the source's time zone. Rule: every time is converted to WAT with the date, and the conversion used is stated.
- 2026-10-26: a sign-up deadline for a pad's trading contest was the real window close for the boycott meta; it was mentioned once and not carried into every satellite map. Rule: the window-closing unit goes at the bottom of every satellite map in that meta, in WAT.
- 2026-10-26: a kit was timed on "this lab ships on Tuesdays" plus a "probably this week" post. Rule: a shipping habit plus a vague post is LIKELY with two sources, never a date.

## Hygiene errors

- 2026-10-26: a coin on a US Mint commemorative story was running hard but carried a 3% Token-2022 transfer fee with a live fee authority. Rule: the ~1.4% rule is checked before the story; fee and authority are reported together.
- 2026-10-26: an eight-figure BSC runner on a car story had a 4.5%/4.5% tax and one unlabeled contract holding half the supply. Rule: tax, renounced status, honeypot simulation and largest unlabeled holder are reported on every EVM runner.
- 2026-10-26: a Base agent-city coin had a proxy that could pause transfers and failed sell simulations for some holders. Rule: "tiny buy and sell test first" is written on any contract with pause or hook powers; the desk never calls it clean.
- 2026-10-26: a Robinhood Chain "Anthropic" coin showed liquidity roughly equal to market cap after two hours. Rule: liquidity equal to mcap on a fresh EVM pool is written as a probable fake pool until a real sell is shown.

## Coverage misses

- 2026-10-25: a Japan-only device launch with a giveaway ran a coin to $65M on BSC and dumped 87% at the draw; the desk had no Japan device-launch coverage. Rule: regional device launches and giveaway draw times go on the calendar.
- 2026-10-26: a leadership change at a social platform produced a meme runner within an hour of the announcement post. Rule: executive handover posts from product leads are a Tech lane source.
- 2026-10-27: a Chinese nickname for a US streamer ran a BSC coin to eight figures with no English catalyst. Rule: Chinese nicknames for Western creators and the four.meme board are scanned every sweep.
- 2026-10-26: a pad's own landing page had five named characters and a mascot nobody had minted. Rule: official pages of the venues themselves are a Market-board source, not just their trending lists.

## Communication errors

- 2026-10-26: a dig led with mint ideas before the live board; the user asked for the live market first. Rule: market first, ideas second, always.
- 2026-10-26: a community post opened with "just launched" when the user did not want an announcement. Rule: no "just launched" unless asked.
- 2026-10-26: a bio was written in CT jargon; the user asked for plain language for normal people. Rule: three bio versions, one of them plain English.
- 2026-10-26: the user asked why sibling names in a leaked naming family were not recommended. Rule: when a family of names exists, the pack lists every sibling with status and the reason for the ranking, and offers sibling packs.
- 2026-10-26: text-card icons were rejected; the user wanted a mascot in a specific drawing style. Rule: mascot over text; three directions; remix the closest one rather than restarting.
